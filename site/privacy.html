YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy, in
plain language.

Effective 15 October 2026

Your app data

Shift + stores roster, alarm, leave, overtime, notes and preference data on your device. Features you choose, such as device-calendar or Home Assistant synchronisation, share the information needed for that feature.

Work Alarm acknowledgement

The app saves the terms version and acceptance time in local preferences so it knows whether your acknowledgement is current. This record is not sent to Shift + servers or linked to an email address or personal identity. No confirmation email or signed certificate is created.

Your device permissions

Work Alarms use Android alarm and notification capabilities to deliver reminders. Keep the relevant permissions and notification settings enabled. Device-calendar features use calendar access, and QR pairing uses the camera when requested.

Home Assistant and Google Play

If you pair with Home Assistant, Shift + synchronises the information needed for the integration with the instance you choose and verifies Premium entitlement. Google Play handles purchases and provides purchase status. Those services have their own privacy information.

Support and your choices

You choose what to include when contacting support. Share only the personal details needed to explain your question, and never send pairing codes or security credentials. A settings-only reset keeps your acknowledgement; resetting all app data removes the local record. Uninstalling removes local app data, although device backup and restore settings may restore it later.

Your rights and contact

Your rights under applicable data-protection law remain protected. The acknowledgement record is held on your device, so we cannot retrieve it remotely. For privacy questions, contact hello@shiftplus.plus.

This website

This website uses no analytics, advertising scripts, tracking cookies or contact forms. Hosting providers process ordinary request information to deliver pages. Google Play, GitHub and your email provider have their own privacy practices. When embedded in Google Sites, Google also delivers the surrounding page.

Read the Terms of Use for using Shift + and setting up Work Alarms.
