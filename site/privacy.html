YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy, in
plain language.

Effective 14 October 2026

Shift + is designed to keep core roster, alarm, leave, overtime, notes and preference data on your device unless you choose a feature that transfers data, such as device-calendar or Home Assistant synchronisation.

Work Alarm acknowledgement

When you accept the Work Alarm acknowledgement, Shift + stores only the current terms version and UTC acceptance timestamp in local app preferences. This record decides whether your acknowledgement is current. It is not sent to a Shift + server, linked to an email address or personal identity, or used for marketing. Resetting all app data or uninstalling removes the local record; a settings-only reset retains it.

Permissions

Work Alarms may use Android notification, exact-alarm, full-screen, vibration, wake-lock and boot-completion capabilities. Device-calendar synchronisation uses calendar permission only when enabled. Camera access is used for QR pairing when requested.

Home Assistant and Premium

If you choose Home Assistant pairing, the app transfers data required for that integration to the Home Assistant instance you select and performs Premium entitlement verification. Google Play processes purchases and provides purchase status. Refer to the Home Assistant integration documentation and Google Play information for those separate services.

This website

This static website does not use analytics, advertising scripts, tracking cookies or contact forms. Hosting providers may process ordinary request information when delivering pages. Google Play, GitHub and your email provider have their own privacy practices.

Support

If you contact support, you choose what information to send. Remove private roster data, pairing codes and credentials. We do not send acceptance emails or collect an identity for Work Alarm acknowledgement by default.

Your rights and local records

Applicable data-protection rights are preserved. Because the acknowledgement record remains solely on your device by default, Shift + cannot retrieve or verify it remotely. You can remove it by resetting all app data or uninstalling the app.

Contact

Questions may be sent to hello@shiftplus.plus.
