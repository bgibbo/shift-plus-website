An overview of the app’s documented behaviour and this website.

App privacy policy

Read the developer’s published Shift Plus privacy policy (effective 27 July 2026), linked from the official Google Play listing. The sections below provide additional information about app features and this website.

In the Android app

Shift + stores roster preferences and personal calendar data for its app features. The in-app help states that device-calendar information is read or written only when calendar access and synchronisation are enabled.

If you connect Home Assistant

Pairing and ongoing synchronisation require Premium. Android synchronises roster configuration, annual leave and overtime with your Home Assistant installation, where a replica supports local schedule calculations. Pairing uses a short-lived QR and authenticated requests. Premium entitlement is verified for the connection.

Read the integration’s security documentation for technical details. Keep pairing credentials and personal schedule information out of public support reports.

On this website

This static website does not use analytics, advertising scripts, tracking cookies or contact forms. Walkthrough and FAQ controls operate in your browser without sending your answers to a server. Hosting providers may process ordinary request information when delivering pages.

Google Play, GitHub and your email provider have their own privacy practices.

Questions about your information

Contact hello@shiftplus.plus, the support address published in the app.
