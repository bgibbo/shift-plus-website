Does browsing another roster change my alarms?

No. Work alarms, statistics and device-calendar sync use the Active Schedule. Change working defaults in Settings; browsing other units is separate.

What is included in Premium?

Custom Rosters, Overtime, Leave Optimizer and Home Assistant pairing and synchronisation. Standard calendar, roster browsing and saved settings remain available without Premium. Current pricing and purchase terms are shown in Google Play’s in-app purchase screen.

Why did an alarm not sound?

Run the 10-second Test Alarm. Check notification and exact-alarm permissions, sound, vibration, lock-screen alerts and battery/background settings. Android may use a high-priority lock-screen notification when full-screen alarms are unavailable.

How do I fix duplicate calendar items?

Check calendar permission, then disable and re-enable sync so Shift + refreshes its own managed events. Confirm Active Roster, Active Unit and Shift Times first.

Can I replay the app tutorial?

Yes. Open Help Centre and choose Start Live Tour or a specific help topic. Demonstrations run in a temporary read-only session.

Is device-calendar sync the same as Home Assistant sync?

No. Device-calendar sync mirrors selected events to a calendar on your phone. Home Assistant sync is a separate Premium integration with secure QR pairing.

Does Leave Optimizer book leave for me?

It suggests breaks based on your active schedule and configured leave year. It does not approve leave or submit a request to your employer.

Premium is not showing after a purchase. What next?

Use the app’s existing-purchase check or retry after confirming the Google Play account used for the purchase. If Google Play is unavailable, retry later. Standard features remain available.

Can I use the screenshots as a live app?

These are genuine app captures for illustration. Website walkthrough controls explain the views; they do not change your roster or set alarms. Install the Android app to use those features.
