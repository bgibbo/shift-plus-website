# Shift + video production package

This is a production brief, not material for publication. Every in-app shot must be captured from the current approved Android release. Do not reconstruct, simulate or AI-generate the app interface.

## Main promotional film — 75 seconds

**Format:** 16:9 master at 1920 × 1080, with a separately framed 9:16 edit. 24 or 30 fps. Captions are essential; the film must work muted.

| Time | Picture | Voiceover / on-screen copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6s | Official Shift + promotional artwork, then a clean cut to the real calendar. | **Your work has a rhythm. Shift + helps you see it.** |
| 6–18s | Real calendar: swipe between months, show Early/Late/Night/Rest colours and an active day. | **For nine-to-five, rotating shifts, and weeks that never look the same.** |
| 18–31s | Real Work Alarms screen, then Calendar bell / Alarm Centre with a mix of enabled and silenced entries. | **Set work alarms around your shift. Book On, Book Off—when you need them.** |
| 31–43s | Genuine annual leave view and a calendar day with leave marker; genuine overtime entry/summary. | **Keep annual leave, overtime and the time that is yours in view.** |
| 43–55s | Custom roster setup or schedule selection, only if the capture uses an approved Premium account. | **Build a repeating pattern around the work you actually do.** |
| 55–67s | Genuine Home Assistant roster dashboard and a brief view of the integration device/entities. | **With Premium, bring your schedule into Home Assistant—your dashboard, your automations.** |
| 67–75s | Official artwork / logo end card. | **Shift +. Your roster. Your alarms. Your time. Available on Google Play.** |

### Direction

- Use brisk crossfades or straight cuts only; do not overlay fake taps, fake notifications or UI animations.
- Record the actual app in a privacy-safe test profile. Disable incoming notification previews and redact any accidental personal information before editing.
- Use official Shift + artwork only as a branded scene or end-card background. Label genuine app footage in internal editing notes, not on every final video frame.

## 30-second Reel / YouTube Short

**Canvas:** 1080 × 1920. Keep action inside the central safe area; reserve the lower third for platform controls.

| Time | Picture | Captions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3s | Fast official artwork/logo opener. | **Your week never looks the same?** |
| 3–10s | Real Shift + calendar with a clean month scroll. | **See every shift. One clear calendar.** |
| 10–18s | Real Work Alarms settings, then Alarm Centre. | **Work alarms that follow your roster.** |
| 18–24s | Real leave/overtime calendar marker. | **Leave. Overtime. Time off.** |
| 24–30s | Logo and Google Play call-to-action. | **Your roster. Your alarms. Your time.** |

No spoken explanation is required. If voiceover is used, it must match the captions exactly enough to remain clear without sound.

## Three feature shorts

### 1. “The week, before it happens” — 12–15 seconds

Open on the genuine calendar, move through the next week, tap a day, then show other-unit comparison. Copy: **“Early. Late. Night. Rest. Know what’s next before the day begins.”** End on the logo.

### 2. “One alarm, one less thing to remember” — 12–15 seconds

Show Work Alarms, adjust a timing slider, open Alarm Centre and silence one individual future alarm if the real UI supports it. Copy: **“Your shift changes. Your reminders keep up.”** Include a small end-frame line: **“Keep independent backup alarms for important commitments.”**

### 3. “Your roster at home” — 15–20 seconds

Show the Android calendar, then genuine Home Assistant monthly roster card, then the integration screen. Copy: **“Shift + Premium brings your schedule into Home Assistant.”** Do not imply pre-made home controls; say **“Use it in your own dashboards and automations.”**

## Required genuine captures

Capture from the current release, with a test roster and no live QR codes, identities, addresses, contacts, device IDs or personal notes:

1. Android calendar: current month plus a short month-navigation recording.
2. Android calendar: day selection and other-unit comparison.
3. Work Alarms screen: Book On and Book Off enabled, readable timing labels.
4. Alarm Centre: multiple upcoming alarms; a mix of enabled and silenced states.
5. Optional individual alarm-silence interaction, only if it naturally works in the current UI.
6. Annual Leave screen and a calendar leave marker.
7. Overtime entry or summary and its calendar marker.
8. Custom Roster / schedule selection, if Premium is available for the capture.
9. Home Assistant monthly roster card with sanitised realistic data.
10. Home Assistant integration device/entity screen with no pairing QR or remote URL visible.
11. Clean logo/end-card artwork in landscape and portrait-safe crops.

## Voice, captions, branding and music

- **Voice:** warm, confident, Irish/neutral English; conversational rather than corporate. One voice, measured pace, no claims beyond the app’s current capabilities.
- **Captions:** sentence case, high contrast, maximum two short lines, placed above platform controls. Burn them into social versions and supply an SRT for the main film.
- **Branding:** navy/white/electric-blue Shift + palette; use the official logo and three-professional artwork unchanged. Keep app footage unframed where possible so it remains recognisably genuine.
- **Music:** optional. Use only a licensed, unobtrusive modern instrumental with a clear licence retained with the edit. Never use a track whose platform licence does not cover the intended distribution. Silence is preferable to uncertain music rights.

## Delivery checklist

- Main film: 1920 × 1080 H.264 MP4, captions/SRT and thumbnail.
- Social master: 1080 × 1920 H.264 MP4, burned-in captions and cover frame.
- Three feature shorts: 1080 × 1920 H.264 MP4, burned-in captions.
- Retain raw captures, project file, approved artwork source and music/voice licences outside the app and website source repositories.
